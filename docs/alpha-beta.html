Alpha and beta

Did your strategy make money because the market went up, or because it was good? Plot one dot per month, draw a line through the dots, and the line answers it.

Move the line

Each dot is one month. Across: what the market (SPY) made above cash. Up: what your strategy made above cash. The best straight line through the dots has a slope, β, and a height where it crosses the vertical axis, α.

Twice the market
Steeper line, beta 2. Huge years and terrible years. Still no alpha: leverage is not skill.
Skilled manager
Same slope as the market, but the line is lifted. That lift is alpha: return the market cannot explain.

The maths

Write x for the market's monthly return above cash and y for yours. The model is a straight line plus noise:

y = α + β·x + ε
β = cov(x, y) / var(x)  slope: how much you move per 1% of market
α = mean(y) − β·mean(x)  height at x = 0
alpha per year = 12 × α  simple annualising, as quoted here
t = α / standard error(α)  below about 2, it could be luck

"Above cash" matters. Cash pays something with no risk at all, so only the return beyond it counts. In the workshop, cash pays a flat 3% a year.

In the notebook: alpha_beta(strategy_returns, market_returns) fits this line and .plot() draws this chart for your own strategy.

The alpha competition

Write a strategy in notebook 07, score it, and enter it. Highest alpha wins, under rules that make it hard to win by luck or by cheating:

  • Same fixed data for everyone. 24 ETFs and stocks, 2016 to October 2026. Every score is stamped with the data file's fingerprint.
  • Build on the past, scored on later data. Tune on 2016 to 2022 as much as you like. Only alpha from 2023 onwards counts.
  • No peeking. The scorer flips every price move after six dates and runs your strategy again. If your choices before those dates change, you used future prices and you are out.
  • No leverage, no shorting, real costs. Weights add up to at most 100%, and every trade pays 0.05%. Beta cannot be pumped up to fake a result.
  • The organiser runs the code. Teams submit the strategy file, not a number. Entries that load their own data are refused.
  • The t-stat is shown next to the alpha. Ties go to the higher t-stat, and a big alpha with a small t-stat is a warning sign.
